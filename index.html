PolitiCard Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

PolitiCard is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that highlights names of current U.S. Members of Congress on webpages and displays non-partisan public information (term countdowns, official portraits, voting records, and recent news).

1. Data We Do Not Collect

PolitiCard does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal information, browsing history, search queries, form inputs, or webpage contents to any developer-operated server or third-party analytics/advertising service. We have no user accounts, tracking pixels, or telemetry.

2. How Webpage Content Is Processed (100% Locally)

When enabled, PolitiCard scans text nodes on webpages locally inside your browser solely to identify and highlight the names of current U.S. Members of Congress. Webpage text never leaves your device.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

PolitiCard uses your browser's built-in storage.local API to store only:

  • Extension Settings: Your on/off and Civic Context Filter preferences.
  • Temporary Tab Detections: The list of politicians detected on your open tabs so the toolbar popup and badge count can display them. These entries are automatically removed when you close a tab.
  • Public Data Cache: Cached public congressional rosters, member profiles, voting feeds, and news headlines to keep the extension fast and reduce network requests.

4. Third-Party Public Data Sources

To display public legislative and news information, PolitiCard's background script makes direct read-only HTTPS requests to the following public data providers (sending only public legislator IDs or names—never your personal data or the URL you are reading):

  • unitedstates.github.io — Public-domain U.S. Congress roster and official portraits.
  • civdotiq.org — Public congressional member profiles, roll-call votes, and legislative feeds.
  • api.gdeltproject.org — Recent public news article links mentioning a selected Member of Congress.
  • news.google.com — Fallback public RSS news headlines mentioning a selected Member of Congress.

5. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

PolitiCard's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs and browser data will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements. All data processing is strictly limited to providing PolitiCard's single user-facing purpose.

6. Changes to This Policy

If PolitiCard's data handling practices change in a future update, this privacy policy will be updated prior to or alongside that release and disclosed within the extension's store listing.
